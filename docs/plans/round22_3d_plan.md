## Contracts

### The `[PART:]` tag, extended (read by `scenePart(fromTagBody:)`)
`[PART:shape:label:x,y,z:size:colour[:axis][:rot=rx,ry,rz][:mat=material]]`
- New shapes and their size numbers: `torus` `ring,pipe` (ring radius, pipe radius; pipe < ring); `ring` `radius` (a thin torus: pipe = radius × 0.08, at least 0.05); `pyramid` `w,h,d` (apex at +y of its own frame); `plane` `w,h` (a slab 0.02 deep, so both faces render; not an `SCNPlane`); `wedge` `w,h,d` (a right triangle in the x–y plane, right angle at the bottom left, extruded along z: `SCNShape(path:extrusionDepth:)`); `hemisphere` `radius` (a dome, flat side at −y; custom geometry, see stage 2). Existing six unchanged. Every size number 0.05–6 as today.
- `axis` as today (`x`, `y`, `z`, default `y`; accepted and ignored on box, sphere, plane, pyramid, wedge, hemisphere, which have no axis; **torus and ring do have an axis**: the hole's direction).
- `rot=rx,ry,rz`: degrees, each −360…360, applied after the axis (so a cylinder on `x` with `rot=0,0,30` is tilted 30° about z). Default none. Numbers written back with at most two decimals and no trailing zeros (`ClickyScene.numberText`); a default rotation is left out of the canonical body.
- `mat=matte|metal|plastic|rubber|glass|glow`: default `matte` (today's Lambert look). A part with colour `glass` and no `mat` gets `mat=glass` and colour `white`; the canonical body then writes `white:mat=glass`? **No**: to keep history byte-stable for old replies, the canonical body keeps `glass` as the colour and writes no `mat` for that case. New replies may write `colour:mat=glass` for any colour (a blue glass).
- Trailing fields are read from the end as today: a piece that holds `=` is a keyed field (`rot=`, `mat=`; unknown keys drop the part); then the optional axis word; then colour, size, position. A keyed field may appear at most once; order between `rot=` and `mat=` is free; the canonical body writes `rot=` before `mat=`.
- **Limits**: `maximumPartCount` 12 → **24**; the number of labelled parts is unlimited within 24 (tags are numbered 1…24).

### New tag `[VIEW:...]` (one or more per reply, not counted in the 8 draw tags)
`[VIEW:exploded]`, `[VIEW:assembled]`, `[VIEW:turn:left|right|front|back]` (a quarter turn each; `front` faces the camera, `back` the opposite), `[VIEW:zoom:in|out]` (×0.75 / ×1.33 of the current camera distance, kept within 0.6–2× the fit), `[VIEW:reset]`. Only valid while a scene is on screen (else dropped and still removed from speech). Added to the four regex sites (`ClickyReplyTagParser:48`, `ClickySpeechPrefetchSupport:43/70`, `completeDrawTagPattern:77`) and to `historyAssistantText` in canonical form.

### The scene line in "marks on screen" (prompt)
`m5: 3d scene titled "bicycle", 14 parts, exploded: [PART:...] [PART:...] ... (add a part with [PART:...]; a new [SCENE:title] replaces it; [VIEW:exploded], [VIEW:assembled], [VIEW:turn:left], [VIEW:zoom:in] change the view)`; "assembled" when not exploded; "it is full" at 24.

### Metrics (numbers and fixed identifiers only)
`scenePartCount: Int?`, `sceneDroppedPartCount: Int?`, `sceneViewCommandCount: Int?`, `sceneUserDragSeconds: Int?` (seconds the user dragged or zoomed before the scene went), each added to `allowedMetricsJSONKeys`, the `Mirror` list and the usage parser.

## Decisions on details

### Stage 1: model and parsing
- `ClickyScenePartShapeKind` gains `torus, ring, pyramid, plane, wedge, hemisphere` (raw values are the tag words). `pointsAlongAnAxis` is true for cylinder, cone, tube, capsule, torus, ring. `sizeNumberCount` (new, replaces the switch at `:599–604`): box 3, sphere 1, cylinder/cone/tube/capsule 2, torus 2, ring 1, pyramid 3, plane 2, wedge 3, hemisphere 1.
- `ClickyScenePartDimensions` gains `.torus(ringRadius:pipeRadius:)` (pipe must be smaller than ring, else the part is dropped), `.plane(width:height:)`, `.hemisphere(radius:)`; pyramid and wedge use `.box(width:height:depth:)`; ring uses `.torus` with the derived pipe.
- `ClickyScenePartRotation { degreesX, degreesY, degreesZ }` with `isDefault`; `ClickyScenePartMaterial: String, CaseIterable` = `matte, metal, plastic, rubber, glass, glow`. `ClickyScenePart` gains `var rotation: ClickyScenePartRotation = .none` and `var material: ClickyScenePartMaterial = .matte` (defaults so every existing initializer compiles; the parser and the self test set them). `effectiveMaterial`: `.glass` when colour is `glass` and material is `.matte`.
- **Bounds with rotation**: `halfExtents` stays (axis-aligned, for the unrotated part); `ClickySceneCamera.bounds(of:)` uses, for a part with a non-default rotation, a cube of half-side = the length of `halfExtents` (its bounding sphere), so the fit never cuts a rotated part. A smoke check: a 3×0.1×0.1 box rotated 45° about z fits.
- **Exploded positions** (pure, stage 3 uses it): `ClickySceneExplosion.explodedCentre(forPartCentre:boundsCentre:) -> ClickySceneVector` = centre + (centre − boundsCentre) × `explosionShare = 0.6` (so parts sit at 1.6× their offset); a part at the bounds centre does not move; `explodedParts(_:)` maps a whole scene; the camera fit for the exploded scene is computed from the exploded parts, so the model stays in its picture.
- **Two columns**: `ClickyScenePanelLayoutCalculator.layout(labelColumnCount: 0|1|2, labelColumnIsOnLeftOfModel:)` (the old `hasLabelColumn:` stays as a convenience calling it with 0 or 1): paper width 300 + 154 × columns (300, 454, 608 — the roadmap said about 600); the first column is next to the model, the second beyond it (both on the inner side); `labelTagFrame(forLabelNumber:in:)` puts numbers 1–12 in column 1 and 13–24 in column 2 (the guard becomes `<= maximumPartCount`); `columnCount(forLabelledPartCount:)` = 0, 1 (1–12), 2 (13–24). The leader start point is the tag's edge nearest the model (column 2's leaders pass over column 1's tags; accepted, known limit).
- **Parsing** in `scenePart(fromTagBody:)`: split on `:`; peel keyed fields (`=`) off the end, at most one `rot=` and one `mat=`, unknown key → nil; then the axis word if more than 5 pieces remain; then colour, size, position; label between. `rot=` needs exactly 3 numbers each in −360…360. `[VIEW:]` parsing: `ClickyDrawTagParser.viewCommand(fromTagBody:) -> ClickySceneViewCommand?` (`exploded`, `assembled`, `turn(direction)`, `zoom(direction)`, `reset`); it becomes `ClickyDrawCommand.changeSceneView(ClickySceneViewCommand)` with a board change `.sceneViewChanged(sceneMark:)` (the board records `isExploded` on the scene mark; turn and zoom are not board state). ### Stage 2: rendering
- **Geometries** (`ClickySceneGraph.makeGeometry(for:)`): torus `SCNTorus(ringRadius:pipeRadius:)` with `ringSegmentCount 32`, `pipeSegmentCount 16`; ring the same with the derived pipe; pyramid `SCNPyramid(width:height:length:)`; plane `SCNBox(width:height:length: 0.02, chamferRadius: 0)`; wedge `SCNShape(path:extrusionDepth:)` with an `NSBezierPath` triangle (0,0)–(w,0)–(0,h) centred, extrusion d, `chamferRadius 0`; hemisphere from `ClickySceneMeshBuilder.hemisphere(radius:radialSegments: 24, rings: 8) -> ClickySceneMesh` (pure, ClickyCore: positions, normals and triangle indices as arrays; a flat cap at −y with its own normal; a smoke check: every normal has unit length, every index in range, triangle count = radial × (rings × 2 + 1)) turned into `SCNGeometry(sources:elements:)` in the app.
- **Rotation**: `makePartNode` applies the axis as today, then `rot=` through an extra `SCNNode` parent? **No**: one node; `eulerAngles` = the axis angles composed with the rotation as `simd_quatf` multiplication (axis first, then rotation about the part's own x, y, z in that order; a smoke check of the pure `ClickySceneRotation.quaternion(axis:rotation:)` against two hand-computed cases).
- **Materials** (`makeMaterial(for colour:material:)`): matte = today's Lambert; metal `.physicallyBased`, `metalness 0.9`, `roughness 0.35`; plastic `.physicallyBased`, `metalness 0.0`, `roughness 0.3`; rubber `.physicallyBased`, `metalness 0.0`, `roughness 0.9`; glass = today's glass settings with `transparencyMode = .dualLayer`, opacity 0.38, `renderingOrder 10`, any colour; glow `.lambert` with `emission.contents` = the colour at 0.8 and `diffuse` the colour. PBR without an environment map looks flat; add `scene.lightingEnvironment.contents = nil` (none) and raise the key light to 900 for PBR parts? **Decision**: keep the lights; set `SCNMaterial.lightingModel = .physicallyBased` only for metal, plastic and rubber, and give the scene a `lightingEnvironment` of a plain procedural gradient `NSImage` (a 64×32 vertical grey gradient made at runtime, no bundled asset) so metal reflects something. A proof picture is the check.
- **Node references**: `ClickySceneGraph.partNodesByIndex: [Int: SCNNode]` (set in `addPartNode(for:partIndex:appearSeconds:)`; a new label on the function). `movePart(atIndex:to:animationSeconds:)` for the explosion.
- **Leader ends**: `updateLeaderLines` no longer uses the pure projection; for each labelled part it asks `sceneKitView.projectPoint(partNode.presentation.worldPosition)` (an `SCNVector3`; x,y are view points from the bottom-left, flip to the leader layer's coordinates) and glides the end there. The task interval stays 0.1 s. The pure `pointInModelView` stays for the smoke program and is marked as such. Works for drag, tilt, zoom and the explosion with no special case.
- **Two columns**: `applyPanelLayout` grows the paper by another 154 points when the 13th labelled part arrives (same corner-kept rule as the first growth).

### Stage 4: the scene window
- `ClickyScenePanelWindow: NSPanel` (`leanring-buddy/ClickyScenePanelWindow.swift`): `[.borderless, .nonactivatingPanel]`, `isOpaque false`, clear background, `hasShadow false` (the paper draws its own), **`level = .screenSaver`** (the overlay's level, so it draws with the overlay; it is ordered above the overlay window with `order(.above, relativeTo:)`), `collectionBehavior [.canJoinAllSpaces, .stationary, .fullScreenAuxiliary, .ignoresCycle]`, `ignoresMouseEvents` left at the default (takes the mouse), `canBecomeKey false`, `canBecomeMain false`, `sharingType = .none`, `hidesOnDeactivate false`, `isReleasedWhenClosed false`. Its `contentView` is a plain container holding the `ClickyScenePanelView`, whose `hitTest` now returns the view **only when the point is inside the paper frame** (the shadow room stays click-through) and `ClickyMouseTransparentSceneKitView.hitTest` returns nil still (the container handles the mouse, not SceneKit). The window frame is the panel frame in screen coordinates; `ClickyDrawingOverlayController` creates one per scene (one scene at a time) instead of adding the view to the overlay container; `removeMarkFromScreen` orders it out and closes it after the fade. Because typing never goes to it (never key), the chat panel's `isTheKeyWindow` and dictation are untouched.
- **Drag**: `mouseDown` records the point; `mouseDragged` turns `turningPivotNode` by `dx × 0.6°` per point about y and tilts the camera node's x angle by `dy × 0.4°` within −60…+20° (positive = looking from above; the default is −20); the auto turn pauses (`removeAction(forKey: "clickySceneTurn")`) and resumes 10 s after the last mouse event from the current angle (`startTurning(fromCurrentAngle:)`), unless the user zoomed or dragged again. The pointer is the open hand over the paper (`NSCursor.openHand`, `closedHand` while dragging) and the pointing hand over the toggle; set in `cursorUpdate` / `resetCursorRects`.
- **Zoom**: `scrollWheel` with `deltaY` changes the camera distance by `× (1 − deltaY × 0.02)`, clamped to 0.6–2.0 × the fit's `cameraDistance`; smooth (`SCNTransaction` 0.08 s). A pinch (`magnify(with:)`) does the same with `magnification`.
- **Voice**: `[VIEW:turn:left]` = +90° about y over 0.5 s (right −90°, front = back to angle 0, back = π); `[VIEW:zoom:in]` = distance × 0.75, out × 1.33, same clamp; `[VIEW:reset]` = fit distance, tilt −20°, angle 0, assembled, auto turn resumes. The auto turn pauses 10 s after a voice turn too.
- **The scroll filter**: `scrollWheelScrolledPublisher` becomes `PassthroughSubject<CGPoint, Never>` carrying `event.location` (the tap's `CGEvent.location`, global top-left coordinates). `CompanionManager`'s sink converts to AppKit (flip with the primary screen height) and calls `drawingOverlayController.fadeMarksThatPointAtScreenContent(unlessScrollIsOver: point)`; the controller skips the fade when the point is inside any scene window's frame. The 0.2 s throttle stays.
- **Lifetime**: a drag, zoom, pinch or toggle click re-arms the scene's countdown (`ClickyDrawingOverlayController.sceneWasTouchedByTheUser(markNumber:)`: the scene's remaining time becomes the full scene lifetime again); `sceneUserDragSeconds` is summed for the metrics line of the turn that drew it (written when the turn's line is written; interaction after that is not recorded, known limit). Escape, an app switch and an erase still remove it at once.
- **Self test capture**: `ClickyScenePanelWindow.setVisibleInScreenCapturesForSelfTest(_:)` like the pill's; the self test turns it on for its pictures and off after.
