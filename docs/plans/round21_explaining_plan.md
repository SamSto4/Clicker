- **Format taught in `skill_draw.md`** (about 110 words, appended to its body): "walking through several steps: when asked to walk through or guide step by step, give one step per reply. start the reply with [STEP:k of n], then one or two short sentences for this step and its marks, and end with [POINT:none]. in step one also write the plan after the words, one [PLAN:k:text] per step, at most 12 steps, each text at most 60 characters. the user then says next, back, repeat or stop. when the prompt says a walkthrough is in progress, give exactly the step it asks for, for the screen now; if the screen shows the user already did it, say so in one sentence and give the step anyway. never give two steps in one reply." One example: `"walk me through committing": [STEP:1 of 3] first, stage the file. [DRAW:circle:120,400:30] [PLAN:1:stage the file] [PLAN:2:write the message] [PLAN:3:press commit] [POINT:none]`.
- **Pure state** `EzraWalkthroughState` (`EzraCore/EzraWalkthroughState.swift`, Equatable, Codable not needed): `stepCount`, `planTexts: [String]`, `currentStep`, `startedAt`, `lastStepAt`. Functions: `static func beginning(fromReplyTags:now:) -> EzraWalkthroughState?` (needs `[STEP:1 of n]` and n `[PLAN:]` lines; with fewer plan lines, the missing ones are "step k"); `advancing(byUserWord:)` for `next` (k+1, or `.finished` at n), `back` (max(1, k−1)), `repeat` (k), `stop` (nil); `promptSectionText` → "walkthrough in progress (the user is following your steps one at a time):\n- plan: 1. stage the file; 2. write the message; 3. press commit.\n- step 1 is done. the user said \"next\": give step 2 now, starting with [STEP:2 of 3]." (and the "back"/"repeat" wording: "the user said \"back\": give step 1 again"); `isStale(now:)` after 10 minutes without a step; `maximumStepCount = 12`.
- **Parsing**: `EzraWalkthroughTags.parse(rawReplyText:) -> (step: (k, n)?, planTexts: [String])` (pure, in the same file) using a regex for `STEP|PLAN` bodies; invalid numbers are dropped (and still removed from speech). `EzraReplyTagParser.parse` strips them (regex site 1); `EzraSpeechPrefetch` treats `[STEP` as a tag between sentences (site 3: the early fetch is skipped for a walkthrough reply, which has marks anyway); the `completeDrawTagPattern` is not changed (STEP/PLAN are not draw commands); history: `EzraWalkthroughTags.historyText(appending:to:)` puts `[STEP:k of n]` first and the `[PLAN:]` lines last, after the draw tags.
- **The local words** (`EzraFollowUpTranscriptDecision.decide(forTranscript:walkthroughIsActive:)`, default `false` so every caller compiles): while a walkthrough is active, the normalized transcript `next`, `ok next`, `okay next`, `next step`, `done`, `go on`, `continue` → `.walkthroughNext`; `back`, `go back`, `previous`, `previous step` → `.walkthroughBack`; `repeat`, `again`, `say that again`, `repeat that` → `.walkthroughRepeat`; the existing end phrases (including "stop") → `.endPhrase`, and `CompanionManager` also ends the walkthrough. The same words typed in the panel, or spoken with the ask keys, mean the same (`sendTranscriptToClaudeWithScreenshot` checks `walkthroughState` after the change ledger and the quick commands, before the screenshot). Anything else is a normal turn; if it is a question ("what does staged mean"), the walkthrough stays active and the prompt section still says which step is current, so the model answers and does not advance (the section gains the line "- the user asked something else; answer it in one or two sentences and do not give the next step unless they say next.").
- **A step turn**: "next" becomes a turn whose transcript is the fixed text `next step please` (so the model's history reads naturally), `inputMode: .spoken` (or `.typed` when typed), a fresh screenshot, the walkthrough section in the per-turn block (after "marks on screen", before the profile). The reply must carry `[STEP:k of n]` with the expected k; if it does not, the state is not advanced (the model gets the same request on the next "next") and nothing else changes. `.finished` (next after step n): no model call; Ezra says "that was the last step." (fixed sentence, through the normal speech path), the state ends.
- **The window**: `EzraFollowUpListeningStateMachine.begin(at:noSpeechGiveUpSeconds:)` gains the parameter (default `EzraFollowUpListeningConstants.noSpeechGiveUpSeconds`); `CompanionManager.listenForTheEndOfTheFollowUp` passes `EzraWalkthroughState.followUpWindowSeconds = 45` while a walkthrough is active. The chain cap is ignored while a walkthrough is active (`followUpWindowMayOpenNow(ignoringTheChainCap: true)`), and walkthrough step turns do not count towards it. The window also opens after a step turn whose reply was shown but not spoken ("speak replies" off): no, then it does not (the gate's `turnWasSpoken` rule stands); typing "next" works instead.
- **Ends**: "stop" or Escape (Escape clears the marks today and now also ends a walkthrough when nothing is running), a frontmost-app change does **not** end it (the user may switch to the app the steps are about), 10 minutes without a step ends it, a new chat ends it, the ask keys with an unrelated sentence keep it. The state lives in `CompanionManager.walkthroughState: EzraWalkthroughState?`.
- **Metrics**: `walkthroughStep`, `walkthroughStepCount` on step turns.

### Stage 4: the Accessibility snap
- **Pure decision** `EzraPointSnap.decide(modelPointInDisplayPoints:elementFrameInDisplayPoints:elementRole:elementBelongsToEzra:) -> EzraPointSnapDecision` (`EzraCore/EzraPointSnap.swift`): `.snapped(to: CGPoint, distance: Double)` or `.notSnapped(reason: EzraPointSnapOutcome)`. Snap only when: the element is not Ezra's own (`ownWindow`); its role is not one of `AXWindow`, `AXApplication`, `AXGroup`, `AXScrollArea`, `AXSplitGroup`, `AXWebArea`, `AXLayoutArea`, `AXToolbar`, `AXTabGroup`, `AXOutline`, `AXTable`, `AXList`, `AXSheet`, `AXDrawer`, `AXUnknown` (`roleNotSnappable`); the frame is at most `maximumSnappableElementSize = 200 × 80` points (`elementTooLarge`); the model's point is inside the frame grown by `pointTolerancePoints = 6` on each side (`pointOutsideElement`); the move is at most `maximumSnapDistancePoints = 60` (else `pointOutsideElement`). The snapped point is the frame's centre. Checks: ≥ 25 cases.
- **The AX read** `EzraAccessibilityElementReader.elementUnderPoint(globalTopLeftPoint:) -> EzraAccessibilityElementFacts?` (app target, `leanring-buddy/EzraAccessibilityElementReader.swift`, `nonisolated`, called from `Task.detached(priority: .userInitiated)`): `AXUIElementCreateSystemWide()`, `AXUIElementSetMessagingTimeout(systemWide, 0.25)`, `AXUIElementCopyElementAtPosition(systemWide, Float(x), Float(y), &element)`; on the element: set the timeout, read `kAXRoleAttribute` (string), `kAXPositionAttribute` and `kAXSizeAttribute` (`AXValueGetValue` with `.cgPoint`/`.cgSize`), and the pid (`AXUIElementGetPid`) to compare with `ProcessInfo.processInfo.processIdentifier`. Returns nil on any error (reported as `noElement` or `timedOut` by the error code `kAXErrorCannotComplete`). Reads nothing else: no title, no value, no children. Without the Accessibility permission (`WindowPositionManager.hasAccessibilityPermission()` false) the read is skipped (`noPermission`).
- **Coordinates**: AX global coordinates are top-left of the primary display; the app has the display-local top-left point at `CompanionManager.swift:3000` and the display frame (AppKit, bottom-left). Global top-left = (displayFrame.origin.x + localX, primaryScreenHeight − displayFrame.maxY + localY) where `primaryScreenHeight = NSScreen.screens[0].frame.height` (the primary is the screen whose frame origin is (0,0)). The snapped centre comes back in the same AX space and is converted the same way in reverse; then the existing flip at `:3007` and the global point at `:3010` proceed. Keep the whole thing in one small function `snappedDisplayLocalPoint(forDisplayLocalPoint:displayFrame:) async -> (CGPoint, EzraPointSnapOutcome, Int)` so the two conversions sit together with a comment on why.
- **When**: for every `[POINT:x,y:...]` target (spoken and typed turns), after the reply ends and before the flight starts; the read costs at most 0.25 s + 0.25 s and runs while the speech audio is being fetched, so it adds nothing the user feels on most turns (measure: a metrics field is not needed for the time; the outcome is enough). Never for `[DRAW:]` coordinates (marks are drawn where the model said).
- **Eval**: `finish` accepts an optional `elementBoxes: [{x, y, width, height, role}]` (fixture pixels) and reports `pointTargetAfterSnap` and `pointSnapOutcome` by running `EzraPointSnap.decide` with the boxes standing in for AX frames (scaled to display points by the scenario's mapping, which the driver already knows for draw records). The tester's small-target fixtures carry such boxes.
